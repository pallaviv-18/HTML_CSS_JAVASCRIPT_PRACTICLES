Audio and Video

Demonstration of multimedia elements in HTML

1. Audio

The audio element is used to play an audio file directly on a webpage.

No sample audio asset is included in this practical.

2. Video

The video element is used to display and play a video file on a webpage.

No sample video asset is included in this practical.

HTML Elements Used

<audio> — Embeds audio content.

<video> — Embeds video content.

<source> — Specifies the media file.

controls — Displays play, pause, volume and other media controls.
